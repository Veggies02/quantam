## Brand & Style

This design system serves technical decision-makers in global maritime logistics: fleet efficiency superintendents, environmental compliance managers, and port authority data analysts. The platform balances deep computational rigor with the operational clarity demanded by high-stakes bridge and control-room environments.

### Personality & Emotional Response
- **Authoritative & Mission-Ready:** Evokes the precision of tactical oceanic instrumentation, marine telemetry consoles, and naval glass cockpits.
- **Calm Under Density:** Minimizes cognitive fatigue during prolonged operational shifts through controlled contrast, deep slate depths, and surgical use of luminous chromatic accents.
- **Scientific Validation:** Conveys mathematical authority and algorithmic confidence through structured telemetry readouts, quantum convergence indicators, and unambiguous environmental savings metrics.

### Visual Style: High-Density Instrumentation Glassmorphism
The aesthetic fuses modern enterprise data density with high-tech marine mission control. Interfaces employ dark, translucent slate layers with 1px precise structural borders (`rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.45)`), ultra-fine bathymetric grid motifs, and focused bioluminescent indicators. Quantum computation metrics, alternative fuel badges (LNG, Liquid H₂, Green Methanol, e-Ammonia), and geospatial nautical telemetry operate in unified visual parity.

## Colors

The palette simulates a deep-ocean command environment where optical luminance indicates system state, efficiency thresholds, and algorithmic certainty.

### Core Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#0F1B2D`):** Abyssal navy baseline, engineered to eliminate OLED flare and eye strain in 24/7 port watch towers and bridge installations.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#152438`):** Base structural card and side-dock panel surface.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1C2E46`):** Elevated popovers, nested data modules, inspector draws, and telemetry headers.
- **Primary Teal (`#14B8A6` / Base `#0E7C7B`):** Bioluminescent accent representing quantum-calculated optimal routes, primary executions, and AI convergence.
- **Secondary Scientific Violet (`#818CF8` / Base `#463C77`):** Represents classical deterministic routing baselines, historical fuel models, and comparative variance overlays.
- **Eco Tertiary Emerald (`#10B981`):** Indicates CII (Carbon Intensity Indicator) A/B ratings, IMO 2030/2050 target compliance, and verified metric ton fuel burn reductions.

### State & Alert Spectrum
- **Warning Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Fuel surge anomaly, hull biofouling alert, or heavy sea state deviations.
- **Critical Coral (`#F43F5E`):** Emission cap breach, non-compliant bunkering, or ETA deadline failure.
- **Border Default (`rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.5)`):** Hairline separation preserving visual boundaries across dark layered planes.
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`):** Direct alphanumeric readouts and critical status lines.
- **Text Secondary (`#94A3B8`):** Coordinate data labels, inactive table rows, and secondary telemetry metadata.

## Typography

The type system separates natural language cognition from empirical instrument data.

- **Primary Interface (Inter):** Applied across global navigational mechanics, alerts, optimization summaries, and modal structures. Neutral geometry eliminates decorative distraction.
- **Instrumentation & Metric Data (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for spatial positions (Lat/Long, True Heading), knot speed, specific fuel oil consumption (SFOC), metric tons of CO₂ equivalent, annealer iteration counts, and raw quantum probability amplitudes.
- **Tabular Figures & Metrics:** Every numerical string across tables and telemetry boards must enforce tabular lining (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "zero" 1`) to eliminate jitter during real-time AIS feed stream updates.
- **Micro-Labels (`label-caps`):** Used strictly for metric classification (e.g., `LAT ACCEL`, `PBN-OPTIMAL`, `CII-TARGET`), styled in uppercase with intentional positive tracking for instantaneous parsing under low display brightness.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform is designed around a multi-pane, dense operational workspace utilizing a 12-column dynamic fluid layout on standard displays and a 24-column grid on port authority command-wall ultrawides.

### Spacing Philosophy
An 8-point base scale with 4-point micro-increments ensures compact vertical density within tables and telemetry strips. 

### Breakpoint Structure & Adaptability
- **Desktop / Bridge Consoles (1280px - 1919px):** 12-column layout. Persistent left-rail system control (64px collapsed, 240px expanded), central dynamic ECDIS/Geospatial canvas, and a 380px contextual right-hand analytical side-tray.
- **Ultrawide / Operations Center (≥ 1920px):** 24-column layout. Split twin-canvas support for parallel side-by-side simulation (Quantum Variational Route vs. Historic Classical Log).
- **Tablet Field Terminals (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Analytical side-tray collapses into a dismissible bottom-sheet; primary telemetry strips compress into horizontally swipeable chips.
- **Mobile Handheld (≤ 767px):** 4-column layout. Dedicated read-only inspector; complex charts switch to summarized state cards and trigger alerts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent planar layering, hairline structural outlines, and selective back-surface refraction rather than heavy, muddy drop shadows.

### Surface Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Abyssal Baseline - `#0F1B2D`):** Background layer hosting bathymetric isolines, map tiles, and core viewport controls.
- **Level 1 (Docked Containers & Canvas Panels):** `#152438` at `85%` opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a `1px` inner stroke of `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.4)`. Used for telemetry ribbons and persistent grid tables.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Floating Menus):** `#1C2E46` at `92%` opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and a `1px` border of `rgba(71, 85, 105, 0.6)`. Accented with a subtle directional rim: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(2, 6, 23, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Quantum Simulation Modals & Inspector Overlays):** `#1E293B` at `98%` opacity with an outer ambient halo tinted to the active state:
  - Teal Halo (Optimal): `0 0 24px -4px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.15)`
  - Amber Halo (Warning): `0 0 24px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`
  - Coral Halo (Critical): `0 0 24px -4px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.2)`

### Instrument Framing
Panels utilize razor-sharp internal borders. When two dark slate surfaces interface, they are separated by a 1px border rule (`border-slate-800/80`) rather than drop shadows, preserving visual contrast on marine-certified glare-resistant monitors.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a disciplined, compact shape system (`roundedness: 1` / `0.25rem` / 4px base radius) evocative of rugged maritime electronics and aerospace displays.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro Radii (2px):** Telemetry trend sparklines, table cell selections, scrubber handles, coordinate pins.
- **Base Components (4px / `rounded`):** Buttons, inputs, metric chips, filter dropdown triggers, micro-badges, segment switches.
- **Structural Containers (8px / `rounded-lg`):** High-level analytical cards, route comparison modules, telemetry modal overlays.
- **Circular Elements (`full`):** Navigational waypoints, true-bearing compass dials, fleet connectivity status dots.

Excessive roundedness (pill shapes or deep curves) is restricted to live dynamic status pills to maintain an austere, highly functional aesthetic.
